## Why the detector was declined

Its shippability condition **failed**, and the instrument every published operating characteristic
rests on is **broken and biased** — research 005's own parser was measured to **delete `high`
severities** while being biased toward them. Shipping would have shipped a detector whose measured
properties are unknown.

## The blocking preconditions, in order

1. **The parser repair is a PREREQUISITE, and it is owned by the research bundle** —
   `finding_recurrence.py` lives only on the unmerged branch `research/005-thrash-detection`
   (PR #267), not in this repository on `main`. plan-059's escalation E-4 resolved that the repair
   is scoped in **only if the detector epic survives**; it did not, so plan-059 wrote no code for
   it. A re-measurement run against the unrepaired parser reproduces the bias it exists to remove.
2. **The re-scope guard is FITTED.** It was derived from the four false positives it excludes, so
   evaluating it on those four is circular. **No 2×2 may be quoted until the label is
   re-established.**
3. **The label must be established BLIND** — by a party who has not run the guard, before the guard
   is run, with the labels sealed in between. Research 005 already names the defect this avoids:
   *"the label and the discriminator were authored by the same agent with no held-out set."*

## The blind labelling procedure, in one line

Freeze the corpus, have someone who has **not** run the guard hand-read the nine bundles and
record a blind label per bundle, **seal** those labels, and only then run the guard against
them. The seal is what makes the comparison a test rather than a fit. Full procedure:
`findings/detector-redecision.md` §2.

## The held-out set (nine bundles, none ever hand-read)

`d3-pxe/plan-015`, `d3-pxe/plan-016`, `d3-pxe/plan-018`, `d3-pxe/plan-019`, `pybridge/plan-006`,
`yoshiko-flow/plan-046`, `yoshiko-flow/plan-051`, `yoshiko-flow/plan-052`, `yoshiko-flow/plan-056`.

These fire under a parser-free reading and are silent under the study's instrument. Because none
was hand-read, none is in the derivation set — so this is a genuine **held-out** sample of nine.

## Two bounds that survive even a passing evaluation

Stated so a green result is not over-read: the detector is **first computable at pass 3**, so a
plan abandoned at pass 2 is outside its reach entirely; and **recall has never been measured for
anything**, so nothing bounds the episodes it misses.

## A hazard for whoever runs this

**Assert a PROPERTY against a FROZEN fixture corpus — never a literal against a live one.** An
earlier plan-059 draft asserted `fires == 22 and evaluable == 43`. `43` is a live denominator (the
corpus grows, and plan-059 itself becomes a ≥3-pass bundle), and `22` came from a parser-free grep
— a *different instrument*, not ground truth. Asserting the repaired parser lands there pre-judges
the repair and creates a Goodhart incentive to tune to it.
