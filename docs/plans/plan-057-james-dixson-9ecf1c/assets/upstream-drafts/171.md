# Draft comment — #171 (yf-okf: nested index.md generation)

**Disposition: `partial`. #171 stays OPEN.**

plan-056 shipped the `description:` **producer** contract; plan-057 shipped the **consumer** of
that key, as `REQ-OKF-012(c)`:

    caller-supplied desc  ->  the linked file's frontmatter `description:`  ->  its H1  ->  bare

The chain **never synthesizes** — falling off the end emits a bare entry, never a placeholder.
The caller-supplied link is FIRST and is not optional: measured, **150** corpus entries carry an
authored member-table string and **32 of 44** research entries carry the `[phase]` prefix, while
only **7.8%** (16/204) of indexed `.md` members carry a `description:` key at all. A chain that
began at frontmatter would have silently replaced every authored string in the corpus.

**Nested `index.md` generation remains DECLINED** (plan-056 D-1), and this issue stays open as
its tracker. The root index was deepened instead. The measurement behind that choice is #171's
own: **52%** of subdirectories would receive a listing of no value.

Measured outcome of deepening rather than nesting: over the frozen 25-bundle denominator the
boilerplate ratio went **0.6848 -> 0.3443**, with 427 described entries and 280 distinct.
