# Draft comment — #170 (OKF consumer round-trip fidelity is unverified)

**Disposition: `partial`. #170 stays OPEN, on both of its original grounds.**

plan-057 shipped the **root-framing characterisation** as `REQ-OKF-034`, a decision of record:

- **OKF v0.2 specifies NO bundle-root identification procedure**, and the gap is **circular** —
  the only in-band marker is the bundle-root `index.md`'s `okf_version` key, which is precisely
  the key a *wrongly-rooted* consumer rejects. A marker legible only to a consumer that has
  already solved the problem does not solve it.
- Recorded as measured upstream fact in `OKF-BASELINE.md` §7b; the yf-layer decision that fills
  the gap lives in `OKF-YF-EXTENSIONS.md` §9b — a yf artifact folder **is** a bundle root, and a
  consumer that roots elsewhere reports **false violations**: an upstream gap, not a yf defect.
- **No bundle-root marker file was added** (D-12), and **nothing was filed upstream to the OKF
  project** (D-9) — it is tracked read-only.

**Why `partial` and not `include`, restated so it is not lost:**

1. The **WRITE half cannot be exercised at all** — okf-lint's `--fix` hard-delegates to
   bookpipe-internal generators and crashes on a yf tree.
2. The **READ half is only partially evidenced** — **1 283 of 1 383** concept documents were
   never inspected. Nothing here may be read as evidence that this corpus passes OKF's B1/B2
   rules. That caveat is now carried **inline in the baseline**, where a reader meets it, rather
   than only in a plan.
