FILED ON ITS OWN MERITS, INDEPENDENT OF ARCHIFY. The operator resolved the archify trial as
KEEP FOR EXPLORATION, so this is not a migration prerequisite — it is a latent defect in OUR
instrument that the trial happened to expose.

`check_web_counts.py` decides its region grammar with a single boolean: `is_d2 = suffix == ".d2"`.
Markdown bullets WRAP, so a `.md` region extends to the next bullet/heading/blank line; `.d2`
labels do NOT wrap, so a `.d2` region is exactly one line. Everything that is not `.d2` gets the
markdown rule.

TWICE OBSERVED, on two different third shapes:
  * plan-066 EXP-001 recorded it as a latent hazard after the region logic broke on a third shape.
  * plan-067 Epic 3 measured it directly: pointed at an archify `.json` the checker reports 7
    mismatches, and at the delivered `.html` 63 mismatches and 12 unenumerated groups — ALL FALSE
    POSITIVES, produced by applying the continuation-region rule to formats that do not wrap.

WHY IT IS LATENT RATHER THAN LIVE: `DEFAULT_CORPUS` only expands `.md` and `.d2`, so nothing in
the shipped recipe hits it today. It bites the moment anyone points `--corpus` at a third shape —
and the failure is a burst of confident false FAILs, which is the shape most likely to get the
checker distrusted and then disabled.

SUGGESTED SHAPE: replace the boolean with a registry mapping suffix -> region strategy, and make
an UNREGISTERED suffix INCONCLUSIVE (exit 2) rather than silently defaulting to the markdown rule.
An unknown shape is a statement about the instrument, not about the document — the same
three-valued discipline REQ-DATA-024 and `plan067_checks.py` already apply.

NOT PUSHED UPSTREAM. Raised under D5 as a normal follow-on; the operator decides at the Upstream
write authorization gate.
