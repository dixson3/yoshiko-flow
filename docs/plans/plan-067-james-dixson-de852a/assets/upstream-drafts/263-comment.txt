**Partial progress from plan-067** (`docs/plans/plan-067-james-dixson-de852a`), commit `1112676`.
**This issue stays OPEN** — the META class it names is not resolved.

## One instance closed

`check_web_counts`'s `not_checked` conflated **"no ids were enumerated"** with **"checked and
clean"**. Two facts, one signal. Fixed by Issue 1.1b (#376): an unenumerated group is now a
finding with its own key and count, reported apart from the clean population.

## Five more instances met while doing it — which is this issue's own point

plan-067 encountered the class **five further times**, and none was anticipated:

1. `sc_architecture_complete` hardcoded `architecture.d2`, so it reported **PASS while its real
   subject `architecture-deps.d2` did not exist** — a criterion satisfied by the *absence* of its
   subject.
2. `render-bytes-match` used `glob` where the artifacts had moved into a subdirectory, so it
   re-rendered **4 of 9** while reporting *"all identical"*.
3. A control-registry suffix spelled one way for two different facts, passing a **label** to
   `argparse` as a **subcommand**.
4. `plan066_checks`'s `FORMULAS_D2` served both *"the site plan-066 repaired"* (about the past)
   and *"the current subject of the claim"* (about now); repointing it fixed one criterion and
   broke another.
5. A `.md`-scoped grep counting **plugin-generated** embeds — a surface that structurally
   **cannot** hold them — returning a confident count of 11 that answered the wrong question.

The last is the least guarded of the five: registered checkers have controls and vacuity floors; a
one-off measurement has neither.

## Why the META stays open

Six instances in one plan, on top of the 11+ already catalogued here, is evidence **for** this
issue's thesis, not against it. Fixing a seventh instance is not the same as investigating the
class, and plan-067 did the former.

**The transferable rule it did produce**, offered as input rather than as closure: *before trusting
a count, ask whether the surface you scanned is capable of holding what you are counting.* A zero
from a surface that cannot hold the thing is not a measurement.

Left open deliberately.
