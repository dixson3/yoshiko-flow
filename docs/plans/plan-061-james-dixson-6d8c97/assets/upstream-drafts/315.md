## Two things it deliberately does not claim

- **`e-readme-desc` is not implemented.** Its predicate is that the README one-liner matches the
  `SKILL.md` `description` *intent*, which tolerates paraphrase and is not mechanically decidable.
  The checker declares it in a `not_checked` field rather than implying it passed.
- **The `--min-skills` floor exits 2, not 1.** At exit 1 the floor is byte-identical to a real
  contract failure, so a sensitivity gate of the form "the checker exits non-zero" would be
  satisfied by a checker that read nothing — the risk realised through its own mitigation.
