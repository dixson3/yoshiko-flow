  D9 — assets/red-observations.tsv CANNOT DISTINGUISH A DRIVEN RED FROM A REAL FAILURE,
       and records no subject. Measured schema: (timestamp, ctl_id, exit) — three columns.
       For ctl-spec-first-order alone there are 18 records, 11 with exit 0, and NOTHING
       distinguishes which run measured what.

       WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND BOOKKEEPING: SC2 and both red-prework gate Conditions assert
       "every control has a recorded RED observation with exit 1". That is satisfied by ANY
       exit-1 — including an accidental breakage, a missing fixture, or a control that was
       simply broken at the time. The ledger cannot tell the intended driven RED from a
       genuine failure, so the assertion is weaker than its wording implies. It was the
       reason a receipt clause for SC1c could not be written at all: nine indistinguishable
       exit-0 records, none of which proves anything about what was measured.

       PROPOSED FIX: extend the schema with (a) a `subject` column recording WHAT was
       measured — the range, tree SHA, or fixture path — and (b) an explicit `driven`
       flag set by the CTL_RED path, so a driven RED is distinguishable from a failure.
       Then SC2's assertion becomes what it already claims to be.
